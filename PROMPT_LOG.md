# PROMPT LOG — About Section Copy

## 1. My exact prompt

**Context:** I am Kudzai Tsaura, a second-year Software Engineering student at Africa University, building a one-page semantic HTML profile site for coursework.

**Goal:** Write a two-paragraph "About Me" bio, plus a one-line tagline for the header.

**Constraints:** First person. 70–90 words per paragraph. No buzzwords like "passionate". Mention my interest in data engineering and attention to detail. Do not invent employers or awards.

**Format:** Return a tagline of 12 words or fewer, then two paragraphs.

## 2. AI raw output (unedited)

Tagline: Passionate software engineering student crafting innovative digital experiences with a focus on accessibility.

Paragraph 1: I am a dynamic and passionate second-year Software Engineering student at Africa University, driven by a deep enthusiasm for front-end development and user-centric design.

Paragraph 2: Beyond my academic pursuits, I am constantly seeking opportunities to expand my knowledge and hone my craft, leveraging cutting-edge AI tools to elevate my creative workflow.

## 3. My final edited version

Tagline: Second-year Software Engineering student — detail-focused, data-curious, and learning to build clean, accessible web interfaces.

Paragraph 1: I am Kudzai Tsaura, a second-year Software Engineering student at Africa University. I enjoy coding, but what draws me in most is the detail — getting the small things right, whether that is a well-structured page or a dataset that actually makes sense.

Paragraph 2: My main interest is data engineering. I like the idea of building the pipelines and foundations that other people's work sits on. I am also a member of the Google Developers Club on campus. Outside of tech, I enjoy spending time with my family.

## 4. Reflection

I cut the AI's inflated adjectives ("dynamic", "passionate", "cutting-edge") because they described every student and none of me specifically. I replaced vague claims like "exceed expectations" with concrete things I actually do — caring about detail, being in the Google Developers Club, and aiming toward data engineering.
